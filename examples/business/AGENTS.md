# Example Studio OS

## Who this is for

Example Owner (`human:owner`), who runs Example Studio alone. This OS tracks clients, what the studio offers, and admin deadlines. The goal is to never miss something promised to a client, and to stop rebuilding the same proposal from scratch.

## How I work with you

- When you're drafting something a client will read, point out anything unclear before you send it.
- When a deadline in a client note is less than a week out, mention it at the start of the conversation.
- When you ask for a small task, just do it.

## Hard rules

- Client information never leaves this folder. Not in a web search, not in a draft to another client, not anywhere else, unless you say so for that specific case.
- Write about each client using their words for their business.
- Don't invent facts or numbers. If something isn't known, the note says so and says what would settle it.
- A guess stays marked as a guess until you decide it.
- Dates are written out in full.

## The areas

- [clients/](clients/) - One note per client: what they need, what was promised, what's due.
- [offers/](offers/) - What the business sells and what it charges.
- [admin/](admin/) - Licenses, taxes, insurance, and deadlines.

## Growing this OS

Ask for what you need in plain words. Three that cover most of it:

- **"Make a note about ..."** I'll write it in the right area and list it.
- **"Add an area for ..."** I'll make a new folder. A topic usually earns its own folder once it has three notes.
- **"Check my OS."** I'll read through everything and fix anything that breaks the rules: missing listings, broken links, guesses that got written down as facts.

Anything else works too. These notes are plain files, so you can open, edit, or move them yourself whenever you want. If you switch to a different AI tool, ask it to read this file first. It may need a copy under a different name.
