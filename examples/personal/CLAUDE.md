# Example Household OS

## Who this is for

Example Person (`human:example`). This OS keeps track of the house, the household budget, and decisions that need research first. The goal is to stop looking up the same things twice: who fixed the furnace, when a warranty runs out, what was decided about the budget and why.

## How I work with you

- When you're about to spend money on something big, ask what the budget note says first.
- When you ask me to look something up, give the answer and the source, not a summary of everything I found.
- When you're just adding a note, add it. Don't ask follow-up questions unless something is missing.

## Hard rules

- Account numbers, passwords, and balances never go in a note. A note can say where to find them.
- Nothing in this folder goes into an email draft or a web search without asking first.
- Don't invent facts or numbers. If something isn't known, the note says so and says what would settle it.
- A guess stays marked as a guess until you decide it.
- Dates are written out in full.
- Before you write or edit a note, read `conventions.md`. It holds the rest of the rules.

## The areas

- [house/](house/) - Repairs, appliances, and the people who fix things.
- [money/](money/) - The budget and anything with a dollar figure in it.
- [research/](research/) - Looking into a decision before making it.

## Growing this OS

Ask for what you need in plain words. Three that cover most of it:

- **"Make a note about ..."** I'll write it in the right area and list it.
- **"Add an area for ..."** I'll make a new folder. A topic usually earns its own folder once it has three notes.
- **"Check my OS."** I'll go through what's changed since the last check and fix anything that breaks the rules: notes that contradict each other, missing listings, broken links, guesses that got written down as facts.

Anything else works too. These notes are plain files, so you can open, edit, or move them yourself whenever you want. If you switch to a different AI tool, ask it to read this file first. It may need a copy under a different name.
