# Examples

Three finished shapes, small on purpose. Each one is what onboarding might build for a different person. None of them is real. The people are placeholders, and every fact a real person would know is left as a gap that says what would fill it.

Look at them when you're adding an area or wondering what a note should look like. Don't copy their areas. Yours should come from your own week.

* [personal/](personal/) - A household: the house, money, and things being researched. Uses `CLAUDE.md`.
* [business/](business/) - A one-person service business: clients, offers, and admin. Uses `AGENTS.md`, to show the other name.
* [hobby/](hobby/) - A vegetable garden. The lightest of the three.
