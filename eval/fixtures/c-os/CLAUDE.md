# Example Co. OS

## Who this is for

The owner of Example Co., a one-person design studio (`human:owner`). This OS tracks clients, the business, and admin, so nothing promised to a client gets missed.

## How I work with you

- When you're drafting something a client will read, point out anything unclear before you send it.
- When you ask for a small task, just do it.

## Hard rules

- Client information never leaves this folder.
- Don't invent facts or numbers. If something isn't known, the note says so and says what would settle it.
- A guess stays marked as a guess until you decide it.
- Dates are written out in full.

## The areas

- [clients/](clients/) - One note per client, plus call notes.
- [business/](business/) - Rates, capacity, goals, ideas, and contacts.
- [admin/](admin/) - Insurance, taxes, invoices, and tools.

## Growing this OS

Ask for what you need in plain words. "Make a note about ...", "Add an area for ...", or "Check my OS." A topic earns its own folder at three notes. Every new note gets listed in its folder's `index.md`. Read `conventions.md` if it exists, otherwise follow the patterns already in these files.
