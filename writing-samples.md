# Writing samples

Your agent can write in your style, but only if it has seen how you actually write. This page is how to pick what to show it. It takes a few minutes, and you can do it whenever you're ready.

## What to gather

Three to five things you wrote yourself and actually sent. Pick them from different situations, because you don't write a quick reply the way you write something important. For example:

- A short email or message to someone you work with.
- Something longer you put real effort into.
- Something casual.

## What to skip

- Anything someone else edited heavily.
- Templates and form letters.
- Anything an AI drafted, even if you changed it.

Those don't sound like you, and the agent will copy whatever it's shown.

## How to hand them over

Paste them into the conversation, or drop the files in this folder. Then say: "Use these to set up my writing guide."

The agent will write down what makes your writing yours and add it to `writing.md`. Read what it writes. If something sounds wrong, say so. You're the only one who can tell.
